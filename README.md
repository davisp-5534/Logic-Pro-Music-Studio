## 🎯 What is Logic Pro?

**Logic Pro** is a professional digital audio workstation used for composing, recording, editing, mixing and mastering music. It offers a deep set of virtual instruments, loops, MIDI tools, effects and a flexible mixer designed for both studio production and creative sound design.

Producers use Logic Pro for electronic music, hip-hop, film scoring, songwriting and full album production thanks to its powerful MIDI environment, Smart Controls and comprehensive library of sounds.

> ⚠️ **Warning:** Always keep your system protected and use trusted sources. Use at your own risk.
